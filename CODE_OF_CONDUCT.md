# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project. Treat
people with patience, assume good faith, and focus criticism on the work rather
than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private
information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this standard. Serious or repeated
violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
contact method listed in the repository security policy. Do not include
security vulnerability details in a public issue.
